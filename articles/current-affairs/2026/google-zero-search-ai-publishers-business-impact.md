# Google Zero: What Happens When Search Stops Sending Clicks?

**THE WEEK IN TECH · NOVEMBER 2026**

Google is becoming more than a list of links. AI answers can summarise information directly on the results page, which may mean fewer visits to the websites that produced it. **Google Zero** is the industry shorthand for the risk that search sends less traffic to publishers and businesses.

## What does zero-click mean?

A zero-click search ends without a visit to an external website. Sometimes that is harmless: someone gets a weather forecast or an opening time. The concern is when a detailed answer, product comparison or news summary replaces a visit to the original source.

![Search question moves to an AI answer and may end without a website visit](https://deshitification.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/google-zero-search-journey.png)
A simplified search journey: an AI answer may satisfy the question before a user visits the source.

## What do the numbers tell us?

A [June 2026 analysis](https://searchengineland.com/google-zero-click-searches-2026-study-479717) using Similarweb data estimated that 68.01% of U.S. Google searches from January to April ended without an external click. This is a U.S. estimate, not a global figure, and it includes many types of searches — not just AI answers. The researchers caution that comparisons with earlier years use different data sources and methods.

Separately, Comscore data reported by [Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/google-ai-overviews-share-us-comscore-490381) found AI Overviews on 39.4% of U.S. desktop searches in June 2026. That measures how often the feature appeared, not how many visits publishers lost. These figures should not be combined.

## Google is changing the search experience

Google's October changes aimed to make links, official websites and timely articles more visible in AI answers. On 12 November, Google announced that its Gemini 4 Hydrogen model was rolling out globally in Search, with stronger handling of complex questions, related searches and richer visual answers. [October update](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/search/updates-ai-mode-overviews-explore-web/) · [12 November announcement](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/search/gemini-4-hydrogen-search-upgrade/).

These are Google's product claims and intentions, not independent proof that publisher traffic has recovered. Search Engine Land also reported early U.S. testing of ads within follow-up questions in AI Mode, showing that search monetisation is evolving alongside the answer experience.

## What should a small business do?

- **Measure three separate things:** visibility in search, visits to your site, and actual enquiries or sales.

- **Check Search Console:** look at impressions, clicks and the pages that attract useful visitors. Google says AI feature traffic is included in its overall Web performance reporting.

- **Publish something worth visiting:** original experience, clear product details, useful tools and genuine expertise are more valuable than generic summaries.

- **Build direct relationships:** make it easy for customers to remember your brand and return, rather than relying on one search platform.

**The takeaway:** Google Zero is a warning about dependency, not a prediction that search will disappear. Track whether discovery leads to meaningful business outcomes, and make sure customers have more than one way to find you.
